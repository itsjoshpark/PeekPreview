# PeekPreview Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-09-27

PeekPreview is a Safari extension that shows a link in an overlay when you Shift+Click it. It runs
entirely on your Mac and does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any user data.

## Data collection

- **None.** PeekPreview does not collect personal information, browsing history, page contents,
  cookies, or identifiers, and has no analytics or telemetry.
- **Local settings only.** Your theme choice (Auto / Light / Dark) is saved in Safari's extension
  storage on your Mac.

## Permissions

- **Access to all websites** — needed to detect Shift+Click on links on any page, show the
  preview overlay there, and check whether the linked site allows being embedded.

Before showing a preview, PeekPreview requests the linked page once from your Mac to read its
response headers and decide whether it can be shown in the overlay or needs a popup window. This is
the same request your browser makes when you open the link.

The previewed page loads directly from its website, exactly as if you had opened it. No data passes
through any server operated by PeekPreview.
